# Breach clustering on the development seeds (exploratory)

Slice 5C.2 diagnostic, generated by `cluster_diagnostics.py` from the
Slice 5C.1 development records (seeds 901–910, gated period (t0, H],
H = day 150; records regenerated with `DT_STRAT_RECORDS`, not
committed). **Exploratory only.** It bounds the dependence range of the
synthetic operating characteristics (`docs/evaluation/oc-v0.2-eval-3.json`).
No threshold, window or allowance of v0.2-eval-3 was chosen from it, and
it ranks no strategy.

* ICC: the within-seed intra-cluster correlation of the breach indicator
  (one-way ANOVA), averaged over seeds.
* Design effect: 1 + (m − 1) × ICC, with m the mean cluster size.
* *Raw* day clusters include the fixed weekday pattern. For example,
  routine breaches are almost all Saturday imaging read on Monday, and
  that pattern adds no variance across seeds.
* *Weekday-adjusted* clusters remove each weekday's mean rate first.

* **Urgent:** weekday-adjusted day ICC 0.005–0.14, week ICC 0–0.03
  (design effect 1.0–1.3). Mild.
* **Soon:** week ICC 0.02–0.11 (design effect 1.7–5.9) for the plans near
  the gate (D, E at the top). Multi-day backlog bursts clear slowly.
* **Routine:** week ICC about 0.01, but with 45 studies a day the design
  effect is 3–4.5.
* **Plans whose rate collapses partway through the window** (F, G, H, C)
  show very large values. That is non-stationarity (the contract
  starting on day 14, or the backlog growing), not random clustering.
