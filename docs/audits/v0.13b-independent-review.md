# V0.13b review: deterministic management alternatives (engine 0.13.0-dev.2)

Independent review of the V0.13b implementation. Scope: the snapshot under
`~/dt-audit-v013b/snapshot/` (identical to the live working tree at the time
of the review; the work copy was identical before witness tests were added).
Nothing in the snapshot or the live repository was modified. No model was
called. Scenario `mini`, seed 52 (the milestone's own decision point) and
`established`, seed 3, were used; no holdout seed.

Verdict in one line: the boundary between what an agent may see (classes A
and C) and what only evaluation may see (class B) holds for everything the
milestone's own code builds; the source session and record are never
changed; branches replay and verify; the slots-closed report equals exactly
what a committed termination closes; the default staffing preview is
byte-identical. Two defects need fixing before the milestone can be called
accepted: the committed evidence files do not match what the committed tree
produces (the two evidence tests fail), and the agent-audience decoder
accepts a projection relabelled as an engine fact, so the decoder alone is
not the boundary its comment claims. Neither changes any number in the
evidence.

## 1. Branch identity

Examined: `internal/session/branch.go` (`BranchIdentity`, `NewBranchIdentity`,
`digest`, `canonicalActions`, `seedDigest`, `Continuation.check`,
`verifyBranchOnly`); `branch_test.go` (`TestBranchIdentity`, `TestBranchTamper`).

The identity is the SHA-256 of its own JSON with `digest` empty, over the
source checksum, scenario fingerprint, a seed digest bound to the source
checksum, decision second and text, the decision run digest, the engine
number, the canonical (JSON round-tripped) actions and their hash, the
continuation and its hash, and the horizon second and text. Verification
recomputes the identity from the source record and the branch's declared
actions, continuation and horizon and requires the digest to match; it also
checks the record's horizon against the identity's and the branch's results
and batch against the record. A change of actions, horizon, continuation,
source (checksum) or engine (`version.Number`) therefore changes the digest
or fails verification.

Tried (witness `TestAuditW11IdentityAcrossRecords`, work copy,
`internal/session/audit_witness_test.go`): two separately built records of
the same run branched twice; identities, horizon digests and branch record
checksums equal. Contradictory metadata, each re-digested so the digest
check passes: horizon text one day off its seconds, rejected ("record saved
at 2026-11-27T00:00:00, identity horizon 2026-11-28T00:00:00"); decision
time one hour off its seconds, rejected ("identity does not follow from the
source record"); seed digest of seed 53, rejected. The committed tests cover
a different horizon, a different batch, a different sibling (common horizon
moves), a label that does not identify, an engine edit and a forged
continuation. The identity names no seed field.

Verdict: no defect.

## 3. Replay integrity

Examined: `verifyBranchOnly` (identity digest, checksums, engine, record of
the same run, saved at the horizon, horizon digests, batches continuing the
source's with `allBatches` through prefixes, decision batch at the decision
second with the identity's actions and the branch's results, then a fresh
`Replay` of the branch record that must reproduce the horizon digests);
`VerifyBranchSet` (one source, one continuation, IDs, common-horizon rule
recomputed from each branch's replayed latest effect); `branchSource`
(checksum, holdout guard, `currentRules` on the record's mode and on its
immediate prefix's mode, `StateChanged` along the whole prefix chain,
fingerprint and time).

Tried: `TestBranchTamper` and `TestBranchRecordSavesAndReplays` pass (nine
edits rejected; a saved branch record reloads with a 5-hour step and
reproduces the horizon digests). `TestBranchHistoricalRules` passes: a
Slice 2 record and a session continuing one are refused; a 0.12.0 record
branches as `earlier_build_exact`.

Witness for F3 (`TestAuditW10DeepPrefixRules`): the fixture
`mini-seed26-dev6.session.json` (batch mode `v0.2_dev6`, `currentRules`
false, state encoding `dreamteam.state/v3` as today) loaded, advanced one
hour and recorded gives `r1` (engine 0.13.0-dev.2, legacy batch mode
`v0.2_dev6`); `Branch(r1)` is refused with `ErrBranchRules`, as designed.
`r1` with its engine set to `0.13.0-dev.1` and its checksum recomputed is
what a 0.13.0-dev.1 build would have saved (the two builds share every rule;
the replay is `prefix:earlier_build_exact+earlier_build_exact`, every digest
verified). Loaded, advanced one hour and recorded, it gives `r2` whose
legacy batch mode is `v0.13_dev1` and whose prefix's prefix is the dev.6
record. `Branch(r2, …, Verify: true)` is ACCEPTED, compatibility
`prefix:prefix:earlier_build_exact+earlier_build_exact+native_exact`, both
branches verified. `branchSource` tests `currentRules(batchMode(r)) &&
currentRules(r.Legacy.BatchMode)` and does not walk `Legacy` deeper, while
the `StateChanged` walk does. The branch is still an exact replay of the
actual record (dev.6 differs from this build by its action set, not its
rules), so this is an inconsistency in the fail-closed rule stated in
`LIMITATIONS.md` ("Records under earlier rules cannot be branched"), not a
wrong number: MINOR.

F9 (NOTE): `DecodeEvaluation` accepted an evaluation whose `cash_at_horizon`
projection was raised by 100,000 cents with differences and digest
recomputed; `VerifyEvaluation` rejected it ("the evaluation does not
rebuild"). The decoders authenticate nothing; the rebuilds do. The
evaluation audience is not an agent boundary; see F2 for the agent side.

Verdict: one MINOR (F3), one NOTE (F9).

## 5. Audience separation

Examined: the `Agent` type (no projection, continuation, horizon, branch,
seed or record field), `BuildAgent` (reads only `Observe`, `ValidateBatch`,
`PreviewStaffing`, `PreviewSlotsClosed`, `Schedule`, `RosterPlan` at the

the template (renders `Agent` only; `Summary`/`Detail` call `check`),

Witness for F2 (`TestAuditW1SmuggledProjectionDecodes`): to each accepted
alternative of the seed-52 comparison a fact was appended:
`{key: cash_at_horizon, label: "Cash at the horizon", class: A1, unit: cents,
number: 1000000+i, when: "2026-12-11", source: "end.balances.cashCents"}`;
`compared` and `sha256` were recomputed. `DecodeAgent` returned no error;
`Detail(proposal)` rendered "Cash at the horizon: $10,000.00". `VerifyAgent`

validates classes, units, keys, labels and words but not that `source`

comparison to a model (`present.go`: "never sent to a model in V0.13b";

leaked in this milestone; but the requirement is that the boundary be
enforced in code, and the decoder is presented as that enforcement. MAJOR:
either `DecodeAgent` must bind the comparison to its session (as
`VerifyAgent` does) before anything is shown, or it must refuse sources
outside the decision-point requests, and its comment must say which.

Verdict: MAJOR (F2); the builders and types themselves are sound.

## 7. Candidate selection

matches the file; `TestPinnedTexts` checks the embedded bytes). Order:
`proposal`, `no_action`, then `without_<k>` for each irreversible action in
batch order while fewer than four, or `adjusted` for a single action: matches
the text. Limit four: `TestSelectionRules` (three terminations list
`without_1`, `without_2` only). No substitution: `adjust` changes one field

with its codes (`notice_too_short` case); a refused adjustment is recorded in

horizon is not listed and not recorded in `omitted`; the text words this as

compares with `reflect.DeepEqual`; a reordered selection fails.

## 8. Template fidelity

`present.go`, `TestPresentationFidelity`, the committed

F8 (NOTE) as in the table; the contract example in `COMPARISON_CONTRACT.md`

Verdict: no defect; one NOTE (F8).
