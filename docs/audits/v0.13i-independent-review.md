**3. Lifecycle contract assessment.**

The contract explicitly represents the requested lifecycle concepts and invariants. Its state graph is substantially clearer than V0.13h. However, several essential conditions remain prose rather than enforced transition guards:

- `schedule_done` must mean the last scheduled draw completed, including when cleanup subsequently fails.
- Each required round-health check must occur exactly once.
- An aborted stage must constrain all retained evidence, including final digests and decision records.
- Unknown draw evidence cannot truthfully imply zero model calls.
- Marker absence cannot establish that the subsequent required directory sync succeeded.

The four disclosed implementation amendments have different consequences:

| Amendment | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Remove marker-removal `after` phase | Does not establish that the remaining completion boundary is safe. The post-unlink window remains consequential. |
| Broaden `os_error` | Unsound as implemented: arbitrary exception names, including `KeyboardInterrupt`, qualify. |
| Require observed health for ordinary runs | Appropriate strengthening; explicit fixture handling remains distinguishable. |
| Defer SIGINT in recorder frames | Useful but incomplete. Reused draw and transport bookkeeping remain interruptible, and deferred notification is lost through the public wrapper. |

The final contract therefore does not yet provide the rigorous common definition requested by the owner.

**4. Recorder/verifier independence.**

The verifier does not import `v13i` or reuse its transition-execution methods. It independently reads files, walks the journal, recomputes counters and hashes, validates evidence, and derives a terminal classification. Full analysis additionally replays committed draws through the unchanged V0.13h machinery. It does not simply accept `run.status`.

That separation is real, but shared assumptions still permit agreement on invalid evidence. Examples include the zero-call `draw_unrecorded` fallback, unrestricted `os_error` names, and marker absence as completion evidence. The shared timestamp parser also accepts strings outside the specified grammar.

All acceptance findings below passed **full analysis**, not merely the structural classifier.

**8. Finalization, SIGINT, and durability.**

The marker correctly protects the original final-manifest failures. It does not protect the entire promised protocol.

A real child process killed immediately after `os.unlink(FINALIZING)` exits with **−9** before the final directory sync. Its archive passes as COMPLETE and emits an arm comparison.

A persistent injected filesystem failure beginning after unlink makes both directory sync and marker recreation fail. The recorder raises `RecordingError`, yet the analyzer accepts COMPLETE and emits the comparison. This is the same material failure class as DT13H-03.

The disclosed limitations concerning power loss, filesystem ordering, and per-file atomicity are reasonable. They do not excuse these demonstrated process-termination and handled-error outcomes.

Deferring SIGINT during finalization can preserve a legitimately completed run. The public API must still report the interrupt; currently only callers retaining the `Recorder` object can inspect the flag.

**9. Timestamps.**

Validation now rejects the original null, missing, unparseable, reversed, out-of-interval, and non-UTC-offset cases. Ordering and cross-reference checks are substantial improvements.

Two grammar holes remain: Python’s `$` accepts a final newline, and `\d` accepts non-ASCII decimal digits. Reformatting all timestamp references consistently bypasses validation and passes full analysis.

Explicit fixture provenance is rejected by ordinary verification. A positive-control mutation changing call timestamps within their valid draw intervals passes replay, confirming that timestamps remain outside deterministic replay identities. Integrity hashes appropriately change when archived timestamps change.

**12. New findings by severity.**

**DT13I-01 — MAJOR — Handled SIGINT can erase completed calls and draw evidence.**

- **Locations:** [transport bookkeeping](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/acknowledgment/stage_b.py:390), [draw finalization/return](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:412), [recorder fallback](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13i.py:609).
- **Evidence:** Four real-SIGINT cases executed one scripted transport call but produced accepted PARTIAL archives with `modelCalls: 0`. At `run_draw`’s return boundary, the captured local record was completed and contained the call; the persisted artifact instead became empty `draw_unrecorded`.
- **Why it matters:** Call reconciliation is false, and observed outcome evidence disappears under a handled interruption.
- **Smallest fix:** Defer interrupts during evidence bookkeeping, preserve attempted-call evidence across dispatch/return boundaries, and retain draw evidence through all finalization paths. Unknown evidence must not be reported as known zero.
- **Witness:** [captured completed draw](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/adversarial/sigint_draw_return/injection.json), [resulting aborted artifact](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/adversarial/sigint_draw_return/live/aborted-draw.json).
- **Model-visible change:** None required.

**DT13I-02 — MAJOR — Marker removal exposes COMPLETE before required finalization succeeds.**

- **Location:** [v13i.py:709](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13i.py:709), particularly unlink at 714 and best-effort restoration at 718–726.
- **Evidence:** SIGKILL after unlink and persistent I/O failure preventing restoration both leave accepted COMPLETE archives with comparisons; the latter also raises `RecordingError`.
- **Why it matters:** Failed or unfinished finalization is misrepresented as successful completion.
- **Smallest fix:** Redesign the completion commit point so acceptance does not depend on a later fallible invalidation write. Specify precisely which operations must precede completion and test termination and persistent failure across that boundary. Any adjustment to durability semantics requires an explicit contract decision.
- **Witness:** [killed-run analysis](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/adversarial/kill_after_unlink/analysis.json), [persistent-failure analysis](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/adversarial/marker_restore_io_failure/analysis.json).
- **Model-visible change:** None required.

**DT13I-03 — MAJOR — Health exception classification is inconsistent and the catch-all admits contradictions.**

- **Locations:** [recorder class dispatch](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13i.py:65), [contract exception table](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/lifecycle_v1.json:96), [verifier dispatch](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13i_verify.py:360).
- **Evidence:** Wrapped `IncompleteRead`/`BadStatusLine` and `NotConnected` produce rejected genuine partials. Conversely, a supplied `os_error` observation naming `KeyboardInterrupt` passes both recorder and analyzer as a health stop.
- **Why it matters:** Supported handled failures become unanalyzable, while known non-I/O exceptions can masquerade as health failures.
- **Smallest fix:** Align wrapped/unwrapped class rules; define explicit normalized exception-family evidence; reject known incompatible types rather than treating every unknown name as `OSError`. Independently enforce that contract in the verifier.
- **Witness:** [health failure results](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/adversarial/results.json), [accepted contradictory interrupt classification](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/forgeries/os_error_KeyboardInterrupt/analysis.json).
- **Model-visible change:** None required.

**DT13I-04 — MAJOR — Semantic lifecycle contradictions survive full verification.**

- **Locations:** [aborted-stage validation](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13i_verify.py:269), [health scheduling](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13i_verify.py:409), [terminal derivation](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13i_verify.py:582), [artifact loading](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13i_verify.py:141).

- **Why it matters:** Legal transition names and matching hashes do not establish a legal execution. `UNVERIFIED` external causes currently shelter structural contradictions.
- **Smallest fix:** Define allowed/forbidden fields for every aborted stage; enforce transition predicates for all terminal outcomes; consume each required health visit once; distinguish absent files from present JSON-null files.
- **Witness:** [forgery results](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/forgeries/results.json), [accepted duplicate health visit](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/extra-forgeries/duplicate_round_health/analysis.json).

**DT13I-05 — MINOR — Deferred SIGINT is not reliably reported through the public API.**

- **Locations:** [Recorder.run](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13i.py:385), public wrapper at 730–733, and `_Region.__enter__` at 234.
- **Evidence:** SIGINT during marker persistence through `run_schedule` returns COMPLETE with no notification in its result, archive, stdout, or stderr. The wrapper discards the object holding `deferred_after_stop`. A separate signal before cleanup causes the entry poll to skip pool release entirely.
- **Why it matters:** The public outcome omits the operator event, and stopped-run deferral does not consistently preserve cleanup execution.
- **Smallest fix:** Return or emit an explicit deferred-interrupt notification and make pending-signal handling at cleanup entry follow the documented stopped-run policy.
- **Witness:** [public-API observation](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/adversarial/deferred_public_api/injection.json), [cleanup-entry result](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/health-interrupt-edges/results.json).
- **Model-visible change:** None required.

**DT13I-06 — MINOR — Timestamp parser accepts nonconforming strings.**

- **Location:** [lifecycle_spec.py:123](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/repo/analysis/alternatives/lifecycle_spec.py:123).
- **Evidence:** Consistently newline-terminated and Arabic-Indic-digit timestamps pass full COMPLETE analysis.
- **Why it matters:** Accepted provenance need not satisfy the specified ASCII RFC 3339 representation.
- **Smallest fix:** Use full-string matching with ASCII `[0-9]`, retaining calendar and ordering checks.
- **Witness:** [timestamp mutation script](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/extra_forgeries.py), [results](/home/george/dt-audit-v013i-review/scratch/extra-forgeries/results.json).
- **Model-visible change:** None required.
