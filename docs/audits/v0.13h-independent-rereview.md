**DT13H-01 — MAJOR — Health classification rejects ordinary failures and permits contradictory healthy evidence.**

- **Location:** [v13h.py:727](/home/george/dt-audit-v013h-rereview/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:727), [v13h.py:787](/home/george/dt-audit-v013h-rereview/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:787), and [v13h.py:989](/home/george/dt-audit-v013h-rereview/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:989).
- **Evidence/example:** Production getter plus standard HTTP parsing yields `RemoteDisconnected` or `IncompleteRead`; the finalized partial archive fails “health stop lacks supported evidence.” Conversely, a healthy/error combination proceeds to accepted `COMPLETE`.
- **Why it matters:** Ordinary outage evidence remains unanalyzable, while contradictory input can be treated as successful health evidence. The recorder does not actually share the verifier’s classification rule.
- **Smallest fix:** Cover the demonstrated getter failures; use one explicit classification for healthy, unhealthy, malformed, supported read failure, and invalid evidence. Reject contradictory error/status shapes before continuation.
- **Human presentation re-review:** No model-visible wording change is necessary; updated record-only examples and the resulting exact package still require pending human review.

**DT13H-02 — MAJOR — Handled completed-draw boundary interruptions produce inconsistent evidence.**

- **Location:** [v13h.py:840](/home/george/dt-audit-v013h-rereview/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:840), [v13h.py:845](/home/george/dt-audit-v013h-rereview/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:845), and [v13h.py:853](/home/george/dt-audit-v013h-rereview/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:853).
- **Evidence/example:** Actual SIGINT immediately after ledger persistence is caught; the process exits successfully, but the ledger contains a completed draw while `drawsCompleted` remains zero. Other boundaries retain stale `stage="draw"` without an aborted artifact.
- **Why it matters:** These are handled operator interruptions, not excluded hard-kill or filesystem catastrophes. They violate both truthful accounting and analyzable-partial requirements.
- **Smallest fix:** Give completed-draw recording its own guarded stage; retain a returned draw before any subsequent failure can discard it; reconcile counters from retained evidence when handling interruption. Do not invent an aborted draw for an already completed draw.
- **Human presentation re-review:** No model-visible wording change required; refresh record-only lifecycle evidence.

**DT13H-03 — MAJOR — Failed final persistence can pass as COMPLETE.**

- **Location:** [v13h.py:715](/home/george/dt-audit-v013h-rereview/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:715), [v13h.py:719](/home/george/dt-audit-v013h-rereview/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:719), and [v13h.py:881](/home/george/dt-audit-v013h-rereview/repo/analysis/alternatives/v13h.py:881).
- **Evidence/example:** Inject `EIO` into the final directory `fsync`, after `run.json` has been replaced. The driver raises, but loading and analysis accept `COMPLETE` and emit an arm comparison. Post-rename interruption produces the same acceptance.
- **Why it matters:** The implementation and recording contract promise rejection after failed persistence; the final manifest defeats that safeguard.
- **Smallest fix:** Make finalization fail closed through all required durability operations—for example, retain a finalization-in-progress marker until they succeed and have the analyzer reject it. Handle post-replacement failure explicitly and test that no acceptable completion remains.
- **Human presentation re-review:** No model-visible wording change required; recording contracts and package examples need updating.
