# V0.12a consequence inventory

Status: V0.12a (0.12.0-dev.1). Written before the safeguard was
implemented. It records what the engine already reports about the direct
consequences of each management action. No engine code, simulator
mechanics, action schema, accounting or scoring was changed to produce
it. Sources are `internal/api/api.go` (`StaffingPreview` and the types it
contains), `internal/sim/preview.go`, `internal/sim/telecontract.go` and
`internal/sim/staffing.go`, as at commit `a267cd3` (V0.11.0).

## 1. The engine's read-only requests

| Request | What it answers | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| `validate` | Whether each action of a batch would be accepted now: `valid`, and per action `ok`, `code`, `message`. Nothing changes. | Every action |
| `preview` | A staffing batch's `StaffingPreview`: per-action results, and the terms and direct consequences of a valid batch. Nothing changes. | Staffing actions only: `hire`, `terminate`, `set_roster`, `set_reading_allocation`, `contract_teleradiology` |
| `digests` | The state digests, so a caller can confirm that validate and preview changed nothing. | — |

Operations actions (`extend_shift`, `set_walkin_policy`,
`set_worklist_policy`) have no preview. Operations and staffing actions
are never in the same batch (`mixed_batch`). The two batches of a reply
are separate requests and are not atomic.

## 2. Inventory

Classes:
* **A**: an exact value the engine reports;
* **B**: a value derived from the engine's own answer without changing
  engine semantics (the derivation is stated);
* **C**: not available from the engine.

| Consequence | Actions | Class | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Termination: the employee, last day, end date, notice days | `terminate` | A | `preview.terminations[]`: `employee`, `lastDay`, `endDate`, `noticeDays`. One entry per accepted termination, matched to the action by `employee`. |
| Severance: the amount and its payday | `terminate` | A | `preview.terminations[].severanceCents` and `.severancePayday`. The same amount is in the guaranteed line `Severance · <who>`. |
| Contract pay to the last day | `terminate` | A | Guaranteed line `Contract pay to the last day · <who>` (`cents`, `when`). |
| Voided extensions and pending rosters | `terminate` | A | `preview.terminations[].voidedExtensions`, `.voidedRosters`. |
| Displaced appointments: the number | any staffing batch (terminations and roster changes) | A | `len(preview.displacements)`; also `preview.terminations[].displaced`. **This is a whole-batch count.** The engine computes displacement once for the batch's combined coverage and does not attribute it to individual actions. |
| Displaced appointments: each one | any staffing batch | A | `preview.displacements[]`: `appointment`, `patient`, `exam`, `priority`, `date`, `start`, `room`, `reason`. |
| Provisional replacement for a displaced appointment | any staffing batch | A, labelled provisional | `provisionalDate`, `provisionalStart`: the earliest feasible slot by capacity alone. The engine says that whether the patient accepts is decided only at commit. |
| Whether displaced patients accept a replacement; lost revenue | — | C | Decided only when the batch is applied. Not reported in advance. |
| New hire: recruiting fee | `hire` | B | The guaranteed line `Recruiting fee · <offer>` (`cents`; "fixed offer table; not refundable"). The engine appends three lines per hire, in hire order, before any other line. A hire is matched by its `requestId`, which is unique within the batch, through `preview.hires[].requestId`. Its lines are the three in the same position. Each line's label is checked to be the one for that hire's offer. |
| New hire: the earliest lawful exit | `hire` | B | The guaranteed line `Earliest lawful exit · <offer>` (`cents`, `when`). Same derivation as the fee. |
| New hire: the contract guarantee per week, terms, start, productive date | `hire` | A | `preview.hires[]` and the guaranteed line `Contract guarantee per full week · <offer>`. |
| New teleradiology contract: retainer guaranteed through the earliest end | `contract_teleradiology` op `start` | A | `preview.teleradiology[]`: `guaranteedCents`, `guaranteedThrough`, `minimumTermEnd`, `monthlyRetainerCents`. Entries follow the batch's teleradiology actions in order. Each is checked against its action's `op`, `plan` and `effective`. |
| Teleradiology cancellation: retainer to the fixed end | `contract_teleradiology` op `cancel` | A | `preview.teleradiology[]` and the guaranteed line `Teleradiology retainer to the end`. This fixes the end of an existing obligation. It creates no new one. |
| Salary allocation change | `set_reading_allocation` | A | `preview.allocations[]` and the guaranteed line `Salary allocation change`, which states that it is "withdrawable at no cost until" a date. |
| Roster change | `set_roster` | A | `preview.rosters[]`. The guarantee line says "unchanged by the roster". |
| Capacity changes | staffing batches | A | `preview.slotsOpened`, `preview.slotsClosed`, `preview.hires[].opensSlots`. |
| Guaranteed commitments (all) | staffing batches | A | `preview.guaranteed[]`. Forecasts are separate (`preview.forecast[]`) and labelled as estimates. |
| Funding test: the commitment and the headroom | staffing batches | A | `preview.funding`: `commitmentCents`, `headroomCents`, `funded`, `basis`. |
| Whether a commitment relies on credit | staffing batches | B | `commitmentCents > headroomCents − creditAvailableCents`, with the credit from the observation (`finance.financing.solvency`). This is the derivation llm-agent 5 already records as a fact. |
| Shift extension cost; walk-in and worklist policy effects | operations | C | There is no preview. Validation says only whether the action is accepted. |
| Long-run profit, patient harm, staff morale, clinical outcomes | all | C | Not modelled as consequences of an individual action. The engine reports direct consequences only. |

## 3. Conditions the safeguard can identify

The three conditions named for V0.12a are identifiable from the
engine's answer:

1. **Termination.** A `terminate` action, with its
   `preview.terminations[]` entry (class A).
2. **Displaced bookings.** A staffing batch whose
   `preview.displacements` is not empty (class A). The count belongs to
   the batch, not to any one action.
3. **A new retained obligation.** An obligation that the engine reports
   as guaranteed and that the action creates anew:
   * a `hire`, with its non-refundable recruiting fee and earliest lawful
     exit (class B, as above);
   * a teleradiology contract with op `start`, with its retainer
     guaranteed through the earliest end (class A).

These were reviewed and are not counted as a new retained obligation:
* a salary allocation change, because the engine reports it as
  withdrawable at no cost until its notice date;
* a roster change, because the engine reports the guarantee as unchanged;
* a teleradiology cancellation, because it fixes the end of an existing
  obligation;
* operations actions, which have no preview.

A roster change that displaces bookings still qualifies, through
condition 2.

No gap blocks the safeguard. Two limits are stated wherever the
safeguard is documented:
* displacement is reported for the batch as a whole;
* a hire's fee and exit amounts come from the engine's documented line
  order (class B), which is checked against the line labels.
