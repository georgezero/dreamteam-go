dreamteam.alternative-selection/v2

Applies to a proposed batch that holds at least one terminate, hire or
contract_teleradiology op start action, or a staffing batch whose engine
preview reports displaced bookings. Otherwise no candidate is listed.

Candidates, at most four, in this order:
1. proposal: the batch as proposed, unchanged. Always listed.
2. no_action: no batch. Always listed.
3. without_<k>: for a batch of two or more actions only. The proposal
   without its k-th action (1-based, batch order), for each terminate,
   hire or contract_teleradiology op start action in batch order, while
   fewer than four candidates are listed.
4. adjusted: for a batch of one action only. One adjustment toward a
   smaller, later or less binding commitment, listed only when the engine
   accepts it; otherwise it is omitted and the omission recorded:
   - terminate: lastDay plus 14 days;
   - hire: startDate plus 14 days;
   - contract_teleradiology op start, scope all: the same contract with
     scope urgent;
   - contract_teleradiology op start, scope urgent: effective plus 14 days;
   - set_roster op set: effective plus 7 days;
   - any other action: none.
   Every adjusted date must be within the booking horizon (today plus the
   booking horizon days).

No other action type is ever substituted. No candidate is repaired. Every
listed candidate is validated by the engine as one batch, and a refused
proposal or without_<k> candidate stays listed with the engine's codes.
The order is fixed by this text, never by any value. The list is a pure
function of the proposal, the engine's validation answers, today's date
and the booking horizon.

Duplicates (the only change from dreamteam.alternative-selection/v1):
once the list above is made, a candidate whose actions are identical to
those of an earlier candidate in the list is removed, and the removal is
recorded with the earlier candidate's ID. Actions are identical when their
canonical form is byte-for-byte equal: each action decoded and encoded
again, the actions in batch order. IDs, labels and sources are never
compared. The first occurrence in the order above is kept. Nothing is
listed in place of a removed candidate: the list is never refilled, keeps
the IDs it was made with, and never holds more than four candidates.
Removing a duplicate changes no other candidate, no validation and no
proposal: a refused proposal stays listed, refused, with the engine's
codes.
