# Presentation v3 and view v2 contract

Identities: dreamteam.alternatives-presentation/v3 and
dreamteam.alternatives-view/v2. Independent re-review: PENDING.
Human presentation review: PENDING. All identities are pinned in
CONTRACT-PINS.json. Selection v2 is unchanged and still caps the menu at four.

Both arms share the system instruction, phase head and closing schema.
Treatment adds the fixed menu and optional combined inspection. The whole
interface is the treatment, including its shortcuts, order and extra calls.
No candidate is ranked or made a default. Directional menu construction
(smaller/later/less binding adjustment and early leave-one-out omissions)
remains a declared limitation, not a neutral-information claim.

View v2 starts from the historical bound typed grammar and maps additional
read-only observation/preview values. Source names, raw refusal messages,
free-text labels and evaluation projections never enter the new comparison
rendering. Public context and the model's own original replies remain in
the conversation. The typed comparison boundary does not sanitize that
whole conversation.

Every accepted candidate lists each observed retained employee once.
Proposal employee references use the original reply positions; S-number
references identify other employees by position in the public staff list.
No-action baseline and candidate continuing guarantees and conditional
severance exposures are displayed together, with explicit differences.
The weekly rate is not a total or a claim about future savings. Terminated
staff retain their contract through the last day; the separately recognized
severance is never added again as conditional exposure. An exact guarantee
is read only when a preview exposes it. Severance divided by two is not used
to invent an exact guarantee because benefits may round differently.
Unavailable guarantees, including unsupported salaried figures, remain
explicitly unavailable. Shared accrued payroll and payables are not netted
against incomparable weekly figures or severance exposures.

Capacity separates closed slots, displaced bookings, feasible replacement
bookings by capacity, and opened/replacement slots. Every quantity has native
units and an explicit current-booking-horizon date window. Termination-only
capacity comes from the existing opt-in closed-slots report; roster batches
use preview.slotsClosed and slotsOpened for the whole batch. A single hire's
exposed opensSlots is reported from its productive date. Unsupported
combinations say unavailable. Bookings displaced are never substituted for
closed slots. Replacement capacity does not imply patient acceptance.
Future demand and capacity beyond the exposed horizon remain unavailable.

The S1-a witness has exactly 2,481 closed slots and zero displaced bookings.
The all-off E04 roster effective November 16 at the October 27 decision point
closes 2,354 slots (1,038 XR and 1,316 CT). It separately displaces bookings.
The mixed termination/teleradiology case explicitly marks closed slots
unavailable. None of these counts is a dollar amount, a duration, or a
forecast of lost patients.

Withdrawal is action-specific. Roster removal or replacement must satisfy
the same 14-day notice rule; effective date minus 14 days is the last
notice-compliant decision date, inclusive, subject to the remaining validation
conditions. A November 16 version cannot be removed on November 3. Allocation
withdrawal dates come from the exposed preview. Committed termination notice
cannot move or be withdrawn; recruiting fees are non-refundable. Teleradiology
starts bind minimum-term and cancellation-notice obligations; fixed cancellation
ends cannot be moved or withdrawn. There is no blanket promise of withdrawal
until the effective date. Missing exact deadlines say unavailable.

The fee proxy retains the exposed relevant-study count, windows, scope,
requested lookback, available run observation period and incomplete-history
flag. It prices historically unread studies at the first matching instant
with the engine's surcharge rule. Contracts share two lanes and employed
readers claim first. The proxy is not a guaranteed future fee or a utilisation
bound. No missing pre-run history is invented.

Legacy reversibility, capacity, guarantee and fee-proxy fragments in the
inherited grammar are replaced in the active rendering. Source references
are labelled in the review package. Only actual delivered combined inspect
answers enter its model-visible transcripts. The full interaction, not an
isolated detail fragment, requires human review.
