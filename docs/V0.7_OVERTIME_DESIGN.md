# V0.7a design note: radiologist overtime costing

Status: implemented in V0.7a (0.7.0-dev.1) as designed, and released in
V0.7.0 (owner decision 2026-10-30). Owner decision for the design:
2026-10-29.
Objective: extended radiologist shifts carry a deterministic, explainable
incremental labor cost, without changing any historical result.

Conclusion: **a compatible design exists.** The engine already measures,
per employee and day, the time actually spent on duty past the rostered
end. The cost can be priced from the existing salary and the existing
40-hour week. It can be switched on by a new, optional scenario policy
whose absence means today's behaviour. No historical session, golden
reference, scenario fingerprint or V0.6 result changes.

## 1. Where overtime originates

The rostered end (`rosterEnd`) is the end of the day's roster shift.
* **Approved overtime.** `extend_shift` moves an employee's scheduled end
  (`endAt`) later, by 1–240 minutes, on a rostered day within the booking
  horizon. It is refused past the shift cap, after the scheduled close,
  retroactively, or where the extra time covers nothing.
  `internal/sim/actions.go`.
* **Required overtime (hold-over).** At the scheduled end an idle
  employee who is still needed stays on duty (`ops.go`, `neededFor`).
  For an on-site radiologist that means supervising a contrast exam, or
  contrast patients waiting with no other on-site radiologist to relieve
  them (SIMULATION_DESIGN §6.9).
* **Recording.** When the employee goes home (`staff.go`, `goOff`), the
  engine books `ot = going-home time − rosterEnd`. That includes the
  approved part (up to `endAt`) and the held part (after it). The
  metrics `overtimeMin` / `overtimeCostCents`, and their approved parts,
  come from these bookings.
* **Pricing today.** `overtimeCost` prices `ot` for hourly staff only:
  the base rate under the weekly threshold, then ×`overtimeFactorBP`.
  Salaried staff cost nothing extra, as SIMULATION_DESIGN §6.4 records.

What a radiologist's extension adds:
* Their reading blocks are computed from the rostered shift
  (`ReadingBlocks(t.Span, …)` in `planOf`). An extension does **not**
  add site reading blocks. The action's own message says so: "on site for
  contrast supervision (reading blocks unchanged)".
* Its capacity is on-site presence (contrast supervision). Reads are
  claimed only when their p100 ends by the scheduled end, so a read can
  also be claimed late in the last block.
* This is unchanged in V0.7a. It is documented here because the V0.6c
  agents' reasons assumed extensions add reading time.

## 2. Actual work versus scheduled extension

The simulator distinguishes the cases the owner raised:

| Case | What the engine records |
|---|---|
| Extension approved and the radiologist works it | `ot` = time on duty past `rosterEnd` |
| Extension approved for a day the radiologist is no longer rostered (a later roster change or a termination) | no shift, so no `ot`: the extension is dropped (`planFor`: `ByEmp < 0`) |
| Two extensions of the same day | `st.ext[day][i]` accumulates one scheduled end; `ot` is measured once, at going home |
| Held over past the scheduled end | the held time is part of `ot` |
| On duty in the extension with no studies to read | `ot` counts it: the radiologist is on duty and on site |

There is no early release. An employee stays on duty until the scheduled
end and goes home then (or later, if held). Idle time inside an approved
extension is therefore time on duty, exactly as it is for hourly
technologists today.

Reading time is measurable (task time), but it is not the right measure.
Extensions add no reading blocks, so pricing only reading time would make
radiologist extensions almost free again. It would also price
radiologists by a rule no other employee is paid by.

**Model chosen: worked overtime, measured as time on duty past the
rostered end.** It is the engine's existing measure. It is the same time
hourly staff are paid for, and it charges nothing for an extension that
is never worked. Idle presence inside an extension is paid, because the
radiologist is required to be there. There is no new timekeeping, and no
early-release mechanic (which would be new behaviour, not approved).

## 3. Deterministic incremental cost

For an on-site, salaried radiologist, under the new policy:

    hourlyEquivalentCents = annual salary cents / (52 × weeklyOvertimeHours)
    overtimeCents        = floor(annual × otSeconds / (52 × weeklyOvertimeHours × 3600))

* **Per employee-day.** Computed once, when they go home, in integer
  arithmetic with the floor, as `overtimeCost` does today. The approved
  part is priced the same way, on the approved seconds.
* **Straight time.** No premium: radiologists are exempt (salaried)
  staff, and the owner excluded payroll tiers.
* **No site-allocation factor.** Time past the rostered end is on site
  and is the site's time. The daily salary accrual remains annual ×
  allocation ÷ 365, unchanged.
* **No double counting.** The salary covers rostered time. Only time past
  `rosterEnd` is charged, once.
* **Remote radiologists.** Their shifts cannot be extended (no coverage)
  and are never held, so they never incur it.
* **Range.** `annual × otSeconds` fits in int64 for any valid salary and
  a day's overtime.

## 4. Rate source

The existing compensation supports an hourly rate, so no new rate
parameter is needed:
* **The salary:** `pay.rateCents`, the annual salary of salaried staff
  ($480,000 for both bundled radiologists).
* **The week:** the scenario's existing `weeklyOvertimeHours` (40 in every
  bundled scenario). It is the practice's standard week and already the
  hourly staff's overtime threshold.
* **The year:** 52 weeks, a documented constant.

This gives 52 × 40 = 2,080 hours a year. $480,000 / 2,080 h is
$230.769… an hour, so 120 minutes cost $461.53, plus benefits.

**The one new parameter is the switch.** `policies.radiologistOvertime`:
* `"unpaid"` or omitted: today's behaviour, and the default;
* `"salary_rate"`: the rule above.
* Units: none (an enumeration).
* Validation: one of the two values. `"salary_rate"` requires
  `weeklyOvertimeHours` ≥ 1.
* Compatibility: the field is omitted from canonical JSON when unset, so
  every existing scenario's canonical form and fingerprint is unchanged.
  Spelling out `"unpaid"` behaves identically, but it changes the
  scenario's canonical form and so its fingerprint.
* **Where it lives in the engine.** It compiles to
  `FinanceConfig.RadOvertimePaid`: configuration, which the state digest
  never sees.
  * A first attempt put it in `Policies`. The state keeps a copy of the
    operating policies there, because actions change the walk-in and
    worklist policies, and that copy is digested.
  * Adding the field changed every state digest, even with the policy
    off. The historical session tests caught it at once, and the switch
    was moved.

## 5. Financial statements and metrics

* **Ledger.** One payroll accrual per radiologist-day with overtime, at
  going home, through the existing `accruePay`. It debits wages plus
  benefits and payroll tax (the existing 25% on all pay) and credits
  accrued payroll. It uses a new entry kind, appended to the enum so that
  existing kinds keep their values.
* **Cash.** It is paid at the next payroll, like all pay. The cash and
  expense reconciliation is the ledger's own.
* **Metrics.**
  * `overtimeCostCents` and `approvedOvertimeCostCents` include it.
    Overtime minutes were already counted.
  * Operating expense and operating profit include it through the wages
    and benefits accounts.
  * Revenue is unchanged.
* **Earned but unposted.** The T90 record's "earned unposted wages" adds
  the overtime in progress of radiologists still on duty past
  `rosterEnd`, so it stays consistent with hourly staff.
* **Messages.** Under the policy, a radiologist's `extend_shift`
  acceptance message carries an estimate, as hourly staff's already do:
  "≈$N at the salary's hourly rate, if worked". The going-home log line
  carries the cost: "off duty after 2h overtime ($N)". The agent
  protocol, its schemas and the action set are unchanged. Only message
  text differs, so a `"salary_rate"` session's action and log digests
  differ from an unpaid one's.

## 6. Historical sessions

* A session record embeds its fully resolved scenario. Every V0.1–V0.6
  record's scenario lacks the field, so it replays under `"unpaid"`, with
  its original outcomes and digests. It cannot be reinterpreted: the rule
  travels with the record, not with the engine.
* A record made under `"salary_rate"` names the policy in its own
  scenario. Older engines decode scenarios strictly and refuse it rather
  than replay it with the wrong economics.
* **Version.** The version moves to 0.7.0-dev.1. 0.6.0 is registered as
  an earlier build (batch mode `v0.6.0`, fixture `mini-seed52-v060`), by
  the established process.
* **Why no separate simulation mode.** A batch mode selects an earlier
  engine's rules. No earlier rule changes here, and the new rule is
  selected per scenario. The economics are therefore explicit in each
  session file, and no mode mapping is needed.
* **Golden references and the V0.6 benchmark.**
  * The golden references are unchanged.
  * The V0.6 suite (`dreamteam-dev` 1), its scenario files and
    fingerprints, and its published development evidence are untouched.
  * The V0.5 and V0.6 LLM recordings still replay: their scenarios are
    the unchanged ones.

## 7. New scenario versions

* **Five variants**, one per V0.6 entry. Each extends the V0.6 scenario
  file unchanged and sets only `policies.radiologistOvertime:
  "salary_rate"`:
  * `bench-financial-pressure-ot`
  * `sustainable-ot`
  * `bench-demand-growth-ot`
  * `bench-staffing-shortage-ot`
  * `bench-stable-ot`
* **Fingerprints.** New, recorded before any run.
* **Suite.** `dreamteam-dev` version 2: the same entry IDs at version 2,
  pointing at the variants.
* **T90.** The frozen evaluation policy (`v0.2-eval-4`) applies only to
  the unchanged sustainable scenario's fingerprint. Under version 2,
  reporting backlog is therefore `not_applicable`. Version 1 keeps T90.
* **Comparing the two suites.** Each entry pair differs in one policy
  only.
  * **The warm-up runs under the scenario's own rule.** The days before
    the takeover build the opening state, and radiologist hold-over in
    those days is charged under version 2. Opening cash is still set to
    `openingCashCents`, but the opening accrued payroll is larger, and the
    first payroll pays it.
  * So a version 1 and a version 2 run on the same seed do **not** start
    from the identical state. The ledger differs, and so do the starting
    state digests.
  * Operations are identical. Their paired difference is the economics
    (the charge, and the warm-up's payroll carried in), together with
    any action that reacts to money. The characterization reports the
    starting states (v07a-dev PLAN §2).

## 8. Tests and fixtures

* **Focused overtime tests** (`internal/sim`), each under both policies:
  * no extension, no overtime;
  * an extension worked: the expected cost, to the cent;
  * an extension dropped by a later roster change or termination:
    nothing charged;
  * two extensions of one day: charged once;
  * an extension crossing the scheduled close;
  * idle time inside an extension;
  * hold-over;
  * two radiologists;
  * floor rounding;
  * revenue unchanged;
  * profit lower by exactly the charge with benefits;
  * the ledger balanced, and accrued payroll paid at payroll;
  * the salary accrual unchanged;
  * `"unpaid"` and an omitted field identical, digest for digest.
* **Scenario tests:** validation of the field; omission keeps the five
  V0.6 fingerprints and the bundled ones unchanged; the variants' new
  fingerprints.
* **Session:** 0.6.0 registration (`v060_test.go`), and a
  `"salary_rate"` session saved and replayed exactly.
* **Unchanged and expected to pass:** golden references, session
  fixtures, the V0.6 suite's tests, CI benchmarks and recorded LLM
  replays.
* **Benchmark:** a suite-run test that `dreamteam-dev` 2 loads and its
  fingerprints check.

## Assumptions and limits

* **Radiologists only.** The salaried operations manager stays unpaid for
  overtime (out of scope).
* **No early release.** Idle time inside an extension is paid presence.
* **Straight time.** No premium, call pay or tiers.
* **The same 25% on-cost as all pay.** No new benefit or tax rule.
* **No new incentive information** reaches agents beyond the acceptance
  message.
* **What agents did with it** (V0.7c, [benchmark/v07c-dev/](benchmark/v07c-dev/README.md)).
  Qwen kept extending shifts with the cost visible: $1,615–$10,154 of
  approved overtime per 90-day run. Its recorded reasons cite the cost
  both as a reason not to extend and while extending.
