# V0.13l lifecycle contract

Identity: `dreamteam.run-lifecycle/v4`. Machine contract: `analysis/alternatives/bindings_v2.json`, schema `dreamteam.evidence-bindings/2`. DRAFT, NOT LOCKED. Independent review PENDING. Human presentation review PENDING. Protocol lock and live inference NO GO.

V0.13l is contract-driven. The [inventory](EVIDENCE_BINDINGS.md) states three general guarantees and seventeen executable cross-field relationships, in addition to the 35 inherited field bindings. The recorder and the independent verifier share only that data; the verifier imports no recorder logic, reconstructs from persisted files and never trusts a claimed status.

**Canonical consistency (`dreamteam.strict-json/v1`).** Every comparison of retained JSON evidence is strict and typed: boolean, integer, float, string, null and absence are distinct; integers stay exact; floats compare bitwise; duplicate keys and non-finite numbers are refused at every depth. Retained request bytes are reparsed independently and compared with the structured request; the request hash is recomputed over the bytes and bound to the call journal; replay rebuilds the same bytes and object. Journal outcome fields equal draw call fields strictly. A self-consistent seal never overrides a strict mismatch.

**Causal consistency.** A failed engine request is a closed `{op, args, answer: null, error}` entry, only as the last engine event of an aborted draw, and its error is the draw failure (`T: m`); a raised transport outcome is the aborted draw's last call and its exception is the draw failure. Every stop message is derived from its code and fields. A halt is reconstructed from the retained calls, the protocol limits and the workflow phase (inside a draw: stage `request_start`, draw and run call counts, the consecutive transport-failure streak over the run's retained calls, the first guard that fires, and that guard's message); only the wall-clock measurement is external. When replay reaches no external boundary, the reconstructed failure must equal the recorded one. UNVERIFIED covers only an external cause.

**Durable evidence ownership.** The completed draw is owned by the recorder in the statement that receives it and its transfer to the durable handoff is inside the recovery scope; a handled exception inside run_draw's bookkeeping after the final digests recovers the result from run_draw's frame and completes exactly the frozen bookkeeping (calls, call count, process), which replay then verifies. A received model response is owned at receipt and returned to the workflow only after its outcome record is durable (`dreamteam.response-handoff/v1`); otherwise the draw ends with `ResponseNotRecorded`, no replacement call is made, and the response is retained. Stop handling refuses (fails closed) while an owned response has not passed through its durable record. Failure retention (`dreamteam.failed-persistence-retention/v2`) keeps the pending row, the partial draw, every received-but-unrecorded outcome, the dispatch count and failed candidates, in `persistence-failure.json` and in the structured recording error.

The inherited states, transitions, stop precedence, safe-point dispositions, cleanup visits, call journal format and completion seal retain their meanings. The seal format and `dreamteam.run-finalization/v1` rename commit point are unchanged from [V0.13j](../v13j/SAFETY_CONTRACT.md) and [V0.13k](../v13k/LIFECYCLE_CONTRACT.md); the finalization subtree is checked equal during preservation verification. No recovery or resume protocol is introduced. Power-loss and SIGKILL recovery are not claimed; unrecoverable archives are INVALID.

Acceptance: no false COMPLETE; no contradictory PARTIAL; a completed draw or received response is never silently erased; unrecoverable archives are INVALID with their evidence retained. DT13K-06 (a genuine failure after the final digest answer) remains a conservative INVALID; see [known limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).
